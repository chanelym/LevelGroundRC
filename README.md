# Level Ground Website

Level Ground is a static website built using HTML and CSS for a nonprofit organization that helps urban youth develop confidence, discipline, and resilience through mixed martial arts. The website highlights the organization's mission of helping young people apply skills learned through martial arts to their education and future careers. Visitors can explore information about gym classes, learn about opportunities to bring Level Ground programs to their communities, and discover ways to support the organization through volunteering and donations.

**Live Website:** https://levelgroundrc.netlify.app/

## Features
Navigation Menu: A simple navigation bar featuring links to classes, hiring opportunities, and donations./

Homepage: A landing page featuring the organization's mission, background imagery, and an introduction to its youth development programs./

Gym Classes: A section encouraging visitors to explore available mixed martial arts classes./

Hire Us: A section highlighting opportunities to bring Level Ground programs to other locations or organizations./

Volunteer Opportunities: A dedicated section encouraging visitors to get involved and support the organization's mission./

Donation Section: Provides visitors with information about contributing to the organization./

Footer: Includes social media links, contact information, and additional navigation options.

## Tech Stack
Frontend: HTML, CSS

## Usage

1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Navigate through the website to explore the different sections.
3. View information about Level Ground's programs, classes, and opportunities to get involved.
